# Narrative design

## What it is

Building a story and a world: conflict with ambiguous motivation, branches and endings that depend on decisions,
lore delivered through scenes, and work with the emotional response of a reader or a player.

## Where it is confirmed

- `cases/creative/02-dnd-module.en.md` — a D&D 5e module: 9 NPCs, 4 locations with escalation, 3 paths through the
  story (diplomacy, force, twists), an antagonist with an understandable reason, and a theme of reconciliation.
- `cases/creative/06-dnd-adaptation.en.md` — moving a world into rules: 4 races, 4 classes, a bestiary, NPCs and
  the first session in the Sleeping Wanderer tavern.
- `cases/creative/01-lorien.en.md` — 4 assistant roles as narrative modes: the same "personality" talks to the user
  differently depending on the task.
- `cases/creative/04-prompt-engineering.en.md` — the emotional layer: a character-muse, a mandatory hook scene and
  personalising the assistant assembly after a reader's response.
- `cases/creative/03-tavern-tales.en.md` — a tavern world where a player's "story" is a game object rather than
  text wrapping.
- `cases/dev/04-echelon-beyond.en.md` — an original world: 18 volumes of guidance (volume 0 and volumes I–XVIII),
  including world history, peoples, combat, script, quests and MVP; the text volumes are about 5,000 lines.
- `cases/dev/03-echelon-mini-game.en.md` — lore notes as a way of delivering plot inside a game.

## Level

**Confident.** Justified:

- Volume: 18 volumes of world in one project, a module with 9 NPCs and 3 branches, and a game universe with systemic
  narrative.
- It is a structure rather than a one-off text: NPCs with functions, locations with escalation, an ending driven by
  the party's decisions, and scenes that serve the theme.
- There is validation with a real reader: the emotional response of a real person led to the character being
  reworked into a new version.
- Why not "advanced": there are no published texts, no external editing, and no recorded playtests — the material
  is private and authored.
